# SMS Etiquette

Use this skill when a text must be sent to a guest for a reminder, delay or table-ready notice.

1. Use `send_sms` with an approved template unless the situation is unique; templates pass compliance.
2. Keep to 320 characters; start with the venue name, then the one thing the guest must know.
3. Send between 08:00 and 21:00 local time unless it is a same-evening change.
4. Include one action only: reply Y to confirm, or a link.
5. Never include a card number, full address of another guest or medical detail.
6. If the guest has chosen email in their profile, use `send_followup_email` instead.
7. Do not send more than 3 messages per booking.

Never: send marketing in a transactional text.
