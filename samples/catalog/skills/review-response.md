# Review Response

Use this skill when a public review or rating needs a reply, or a guest asks how we respond to feedback online.

1. Fetch recent reviews with `fetch_reviews` and read the one to answer plus two on the same topic.
2. Reply within 48 hours; thank the guest, mention one specific detail and avoid copy-paste text.
3. For negative reviews: acknowledge, apologise once and offer a private channel; never argue facts.
4. Never publish names, booking times or medical details; use the case id in the private message.
5. Raise a ticket with `create_support_ticket` if the review reports a safety or allergy event.
6. Reviews of 4 stars and above get a short thanks.
7. Track recurring themes like noise and escalate a pattern of 3 similar reviews.

Escalate when: the review mentions an allergic reaction, harassment or a legal threat.
Never: offer compensation in a public reply.
