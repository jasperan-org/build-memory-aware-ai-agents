# Child-Friendly Dining

Use this skill when the group includes babies or children and needs a family-appropriate venue.

1. Ask for the number and the ages of children and the time they eat; early dinner at 17:30 to 18:30 works best.
2. Search with `restaurant_search` and check `get_restaurant_menu` with course kids.
3. Confirm high chairs, changing facilities and a pram space; add them as booking notes.
4. Use `get_venue_ambience` for early dinner; a noisy venue is fine here.
5. Ask about allergies for each child and run the allergen-safety-check skill if any are present.
6. Offer a table near the exit or the patio for restless toddlers.
7. Reserve with `reserve_table` and confirm by `send_sms`.

Never: book a bar or a late tasting menu for children.
